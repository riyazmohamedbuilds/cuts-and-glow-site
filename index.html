The Salon

Craft, care and confidence in every chair.

Cuts & Glow is Thiruvarur's unisex salon and academy, built on one idea: great style should feel personal, not rushed. Every service is handled by trained stylists who treat your hair and skin the way they'd treat their own — with patience, precision and an eye for what actually suits you.

Beyond the chair, we run a working academy — so the techniques you receive are the same ones we teach, kept current and constantly refined.
